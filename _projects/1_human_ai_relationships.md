This research examines AI companions, relational agents, and AI-generated personas as emerging social actors. I study how design cues, platform mechanisms, and cultural norms shape attachment, intimacy, agency, and relational conflict.

Current work combines platform observation, computational analysis of user-generated content, and interviews to investigate para-romantic relationships between middle-aged and older women and AI-generated romantic personas on short-video platforms.
