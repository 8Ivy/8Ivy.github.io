I design and evaluate VR and tangible systems that combine visual guidance, physiological sensing, and haptic feedback. This work investigates how interaction modes and multisensory mappings influence mindfulness training, emotional regulation, and user experience.

Methods include co-design, controlled experiments, physiological data analysis, and iterative prototyping with Unity, Arduino, and wearable sensors.
