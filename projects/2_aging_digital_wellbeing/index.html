Aging, Intimacy, and Digital Well-being

Inclusive technologies that recognize older adults' agency, emotional lives, and diverse relationships.

This work studies later-life technology use beyond care and risk. It focuses on older adults’ emotional needs, romantic expression, social connection, and agency in encounters with AI-mediated systems.

My broader goal is to develop empirically grounded design knowledge for technologies that support well-being without reducing older adults to passive recipients of assistance.
