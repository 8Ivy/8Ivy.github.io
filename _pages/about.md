I am **Yuanlinxi Li (Ivy)**, a PhD candidate in the Future Design School at the [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. Qingchuan Li.

My research examines how people form, interpret, and negotiate relationships with AI. I am particularly interested in how AI-mediated experiences shape intimacy, agency, emotional well-being, and social connection across the lifespan. My work combines mixed-methods research, computational analysis of online behavior, and the design and evaluation of interactive systems.

## Research interests

- **Human-AI relationships:** relational dynamics, AI companionship, and socially intelligent systems.
- **Aging, intimacy, and digital well-being:** later-life emotional needs, agency, and inclusive technology design.
- **Embodied and immersive interaction:** multisensory VR experiences for mindfulness, emotion regulation, and social connection.

I use interviews, surveys, experiments, co-design, platform data analysis, and system prototyping. I am open to research collaborations in HCI, CSCW, and human-centered AI.
